CHAPTER I · 2026

THE
WANDERER

A story for those who never belonged to one road.
He left without a map,
carrying only the clothes on his back
and a sky full of places
he had never seen. Some people search for a home.
Some become one along the way.
CHAPTER II · 2026

THE
MIDNIGHT

A story for the hours that belong to no one.
At midnight,
the city forgets its name.
The streets grow quiet,
and the heart remembers
what daylight made it hide. Somewhere, a window stays awake.
Somewhere, a lonely heart
mistakes the moon for company. And for a moment,
the darkness feels less like an ending
and more like a place
where forgotten things come alive. Some things are only beautiful in the dark.
